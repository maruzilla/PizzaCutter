## Overview:
This AR app simply cuts pizza, cake, etc. into even, precise slices. That's all!
Just point your smartphone camera at the pizza and cut it while monitoring the on-screen guidelines.

<img src="./images/PizzaCutter-1-s.jpg" alt="PizzaCutter-1-s" width="256">


## Usage:
* Double tap to switch between circular and rectangular modes.
    (And with sound effects! lol)

* Change the color of the guideline depending on the horizontal position you tap.
    (From left to right: Black -> Navy -> Orange -> Magenta -> Green -> Yello -> Cyan -> White)

<img src="./images/PizzaCutter-3-s.jpg" alt="PizzaCutter-3-s" width="256">

* Swipe vertically to specify how many equal parts to divide.
    (Range is 2 to 20)


* Hold your smartphone camera directly above the pizza and cut along the guidelines.

<img src="./images/PizzaCutter-2-s.jpg" alt="PizzaCutter-2-s" width="256">
